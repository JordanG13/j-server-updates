## Installation Notes:
1. Click on the "Modrinth" link above to download the launcher
2. Extract, and then open the launcher (elyprismlauncher.exe)
3. Click `Add Instance` in the top left, then click `Import`
4. Click `Browse`, and then find the SUPERIOR - J-RPG zip you downloaded above
5. Click OK
6. All done! Double click to run the game

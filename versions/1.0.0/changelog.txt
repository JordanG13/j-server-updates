## Installation Notes:
1. Click on the "Modrinth" link above to download the launcher
1. Extract, and then open the launcher (elyprismlauncher.exe)
1. Click `Add Instance` in the top left, then click `Import`
1. Click `Browse`, and then find the J-RPG_Souls-like zip you downloaded above
1. Click OK
1. All done! Double click to run the game
